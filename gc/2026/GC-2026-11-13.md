# UCP Governing Council (GC) Meeting 2026-11-13

**Led by:** Amit Handa

## Attendance

- **Present:** Amit Handa, Ilya Grigorik, Twum Djin

## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** Operational Guides for Upcoming DTCs
  - **Notes:** Discussed operational templates and onboarding guides (such as meeting note templates and operating procedures) for upcoming Domain Tech Councils.
  - **Agreement:** GC members agreed to create a shared playbook for all new DTC sponsors and leads to ensure consistency across councils.

- **Topic:** UCP Contributor Summit Recap
  - **Notes:** Recapped key themes from the UCP Contributor Summit, including vertical strategy and personal agent strategy (boundary between PAP and UCP).
  - **Agreement:** On vertical strategy, GC members aligned on incubating lightweight schemas that leverage common UCP design patterns. On personal agent strategy, GC members agreed to explore any overlaps between PAP and UCP more closely to avoid fragmentation.

## 2. Open Discussions & Decisions

- **Topic:** Local Services Domain Tech Council (DTC) Nominations
  - **Notes:** Reviewed the nomination announcement and timeline for the Local Services DTC following charter approval.
  - **Agreement:** GC approved opening nominations for the founding Local Services Tech Council cohort.
  - **Context:** [[DTC Charter]: Local Services](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/922), [Inviting nominations for UCP Local Services Tech Council](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/discussions/1093)

- **Topic:** B2B Procurement Domain Tech Council (DTC) Nominations
  - **Notes:** Following completion of the meeting between the GC and the charter proposers and GC charter approval, reviewed the nomination announcement and timeline for the B2B Procurement DTC.
  - **Agreement:** GC approved opening nominations for the founding B2B Procurement Tech Council cohort.
  - **Context:** [[DTC Charter]: B2B Procurement](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/947), [Inviting nominations for UCP B2B Procurement Tech Council](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/discussions/1094)

- **Topic:** Regional Roadmaps and Findings Domain Working Groups (APAC, EMEA, and LATAM)
  - **Notes:** Reviewed draft Domain Working Group (DWG) charter proposals for regional roadmaps and gap findings across APAC, EMEA, and LATAM.
  - **Agreement:** GC members agreed in principle with the proposals and are completing a final review before publishing the charters.
