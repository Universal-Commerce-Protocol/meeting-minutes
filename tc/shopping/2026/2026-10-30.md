## 2. Open Discussions & PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Product Feed Schema and Native Checkout Eligibility
  - **Notes:** James Andersen raised whether a native checkout eligibility boolean attribute should be included in the product schema for bulk catalog feeds.
  - **Discussion:** James Andersen and Julia Downey noted merchants want granular control over which products are exposed for autonomous agent purchase due to compliance or high-demand inventory constraints. Amit Handa strongly cautioned against tightly coupling catalog feeds with cart and checkout capabilities, advocating for architectural separation so merchants can ingest feeds independently. Ilya Grigorik clarified that product identifiers must remain interoperable across protocol layers, whereas program-level eligibility should be expressed via platform extensions rather than baked into feed schemas.
  - **Decision:** The council agreed that native checkout eligibility should not be modeled as an overloaded boolean in the feed schema, deferring capability coupling rules to a dedicated working session.
  - **Context:** [feat: [DRAFT] feed capability for bulk catalog access](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/866)
- **Topic:** Item Shape and Strike-Through Pricing
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith presented pull request 1016, adding an optional response-only `list_price` field to the item schema across cart, checkout, and orders to support strikethrough pricing displays.
  - **Discussion:** Amit Handa observed that while optional `list_price` support is straightforward from a business perspective, platform implementations face significant European Union compliance nuances and pricing gotchas. Amit Handa committed to drafting documentation clarifications detailing price discount rules and snapshot semantics.
  - **Context:** [feat: add optional response-only list_price to item schema](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/1016), [[Proposal]: Return `list_price` in Cart, Checkout, and Order item responses](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/751)
- **Topic:** Search Recommendations and Swaps Scope Simplification
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa proposed downscoping the recommendations and swaps capability exclusively to search operations, allowing platforms to invoke independent search calls for recommendations or substitutes against product identifiers or queries.
  - **Discussion:** Amit Handa explained that removing lookup and get-product extensions eliminates payload bloat and avoids complex extension nesting, aligning with Shopify's production pattern. Greg Smith invited James Andersen and council members to review the simplified search design ahead of a dedicated Monday deep dive.
  - **Decision:** The proposal to restrict initial recommendation and swap features exclusively to search capabilities was tabled for Monday peer review.
  - **Context:** [feat(catalog): add purpose and reference_ids to catalog search](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/902), [feat(catalog): similarity search, recommendations & substitute discovery](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/974), [feat(catalog): dev.ucp.shopping.catalog.similarity extension](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/977)
- **Topic:** Identity Linking Downscope
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith reported that a revised, downscoped identity linking pull request 998 was submitted, permitting email matching when the identity provider is the authoritative mailbox domain.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik agreed to review the simplified pull request ahead of the next council review.
  - **Context:** [feat(identity-linking): permit email matching when the issuer is the mailbox authority](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/998), [docs(identity-linking): don't presume a client registration model](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/985)
- **Topic:** Business Profiles Architecture and Pull Request Series
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith introduced a modular pull request series establishing a common `business_summary` shape in pull request 1017, accompanied by contextual PRs adding seller attribution to cart, checkout, order (PR 1018), and catalog search and lookup (PR 1019).
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik questioned fragmenting the profile definition across separate pull requests, arguing that different protocol surfaces require distinct data fidelity and that shapes should be composed forward from concrete use cases rather than chiseled backward from a monolithic model. Greg Smith agreed to re-evaluate the linkage between abstract shapes and practical usage, offering to conduct a design walkthrough during the Monday deep dive.
  - **Decision:** The modular pull request series for business profiles was left open for architectural alignment and review during the Monday deep dive.
  - **Context:** [feat(common): add business_summary and contact types](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/1017), [feat(shopping): add seller attribution to cart, checkout, and order](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/1018), [feat(catalog): add normalized sellers map and seller_id references](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/1019), [feat: [RFC] Business Identity](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/939)
- **Topic:** Payments Technical Council: Empty Payment Instruments for Out-of-Band Payments
  - **Notes:** Daniel Wyckoff and Drew Olson raised a concern from the Payments Technical Council (PTC) regarding pull request 881, which introduced normative guidance permitting an empty payment object on checkout completion for deferred payment terms.
  - **Discussion:** Drew Olson explained that the PTC agreed to revert the empty object change and instead introduce an explicit, lightweight payment handler into the core schema to cleanly represent out-of-band payments. Jing Li noted the issue originated from review comments on the lodging booking capability (PR 780), leading to issue 857. Drew Olson and Amit Handa confirmed that the PTC will review a draft handler specification before presenting the proposal to the Shopping Tech Council.
  - **Context:** [docs(payment-terms): clarify empty payment object on complete for deferred terms](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/881), [[Bug]: Payment Terms extension may introduce an edge case where payment can be {} on complete](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/857), [feat(payments): split payments across deferred schedules](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/987)
