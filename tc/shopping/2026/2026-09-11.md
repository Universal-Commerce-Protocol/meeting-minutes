# UCP Shopping Tech Council (STC) Meeting - 2026-09-11

**Led by:** Greg Smith

## Attendance

- **Present:** Greg Smith, Amit Handa, Ilya Grigorik, James Andersen, Caroline George, Jing Li, Julia Downey, Lee Richmond, Scot DeDeo

## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** Staffing Change and Sabbatical Coverage
  - **Notes:** Scot DeDeo announced an eight-week sabbatical beginning the following week and introduced Julia Downey as their replacement for the duration, representing Salesforce as the in-house UCP expert.
- **Topic:** Roadmap Priorities and Milestone Seeding
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith reported substantial engagement on the priorities and milestones document, producing a lengthy list of proposed Q4 items. Working with the Governing Council, he will set the Q4 milestone and also seed the first 2027 milestone so the council builds the habit of maintaining a backlog. Greg Smith emphasized that bandwidth for tooling must be allocated explicitly or it will not remain a priority.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik presented a filtered list of high-demand items from Shopify's backlog: the `ask` capability, async and progressive checkout to reduce UI and UX latency, constrained checkout fields, recurring commerce and subscriptions, missing bundle primitives, add-on products, refactoring the availability object, price reductions for catalog advertising, identity linking and privacy hardening for agent runtimes, and obfuscated terms for gifting and registry workflows. Amit Handa supported publishing the combined priority list externally to invite ecosystem contribution. James Andersen raised identity-linked buyer data as a gap; Ilya Grigorik confirmed it is underspecified and requires explicitly defined expected behaviors. Ilya Grigorik and Amit Handa advised collecting shared priorities without making rigid external commitments, with participants bringing prioritization signals back the following week.
- **Topic:** UCP Summit Agenda and Structure
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith and Caroline George outlined the summit: day one focused on 2027 priorities and day two on building with UCP, including 75-minute breakout tracks and a panel discussion. Greg Smith confirmed day two will center on the implementer experience to help shape 2027 work, building on Ilya Grigorik's report from a Zurich presentation covering UCP and WebMCP pain points that Amit Handa noted was effective at raising awareness of protocol failures.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik proposed incorporating unconference-style hack days and hands-on issue workshops, and Amit Handa suggested gathering implementer pain points on day one. James Andersen and Amit Handa recommended including platform builders and representatives from Stripe and Salesforce on panels, to represent companies building UCP implementations on behalf of merchants.
- **Topic:** Open Source Success Metrics Framework
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith introduced a framework for measuring open source UCP success without relying on proprietary metrics, covering adoption, developer experience, and community engagement. Ilya Grigorik suggested leveraging the Google-sponsored HTTP Archive to probe for well-known UCP endpoints and generate adoption statistics at no cost. On developer experience, Ilya Grigorik and Greg Smith discussed shifting away from SDK download counts toward clear API specifications, since agents and bespoke architectures reduce the utility of vanilla SDKs; Amit Handa noted internal research showing merchants reporting that the SDKs are currently broken.
  - **Discussion:** Greg Smith proposed a community engagement metric based on the percentage of specification pull request approvals by maintainers unaffiliated with founding entities. Amit Handa advised relaxing the threshold slightly to preserve safeguards while expanding participation. Ilya Grigorik shared prior analysis of public GitHub data covering pairwise reviewer-contributor relationships by company, and the group agreed to share contribution statistics transparently, potentially via a daily refreshing dashboard.
  - **Decision:** The group agreed to adopt the proposed success metrics as a baseline to iterate on.
- **Topic:** Bulk Feeds Domain Working Group Progress
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik reported the Feeds working group is decomposing the problem space, establishing milestones, and enumerating authentication and formatting requirements, and remains in the divergence and discovery phase.
  - **Context:** [RFC: A Bulk Product Discovery Capability](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/550)

## 2. Open Discussions & PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Media Variants
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik urged the group to review the media pull request, noting it is backward compatible, already shipped in Shopify's catalog, and should be incorporated into the booking proposal to support video and 3D tours.
  - **Context:** [feat(media): model image, video, and 3D variants](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/690)
- **Topic:** Ask Capability
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa highlighted the `ask` capability as a high-priority feature, enabling business agents to communicate over UCP without connecting entire ERP and CRM systems, with strong demand from retailers including Target and Walmart. Ilya Grigorik explained the design evolved from the limitations of Shopify's storefront MCP tools, combining unstructured queries with structured outputs such as references. Amit Handa committed to reviewing the pull request.
  - **Context:** [feat: `ask` capability for natural-language Q&A](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/538)
- **Topic:** Return and Cancellation Policies
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa presented the updated return policy pull request with added structure. Ilya Grigorik suggested comparing it against the cancellation policies in the lodging pull request, but Jing Li and Amit Handa recommended keeping returns and cancellation policies separate initially, given shopping-specific divergences, before identifying commonalities.
  - **Decision:** The group agreed to treat returns and lodging cancellation policies as separate policies initially, and to evaluate commonalities afterward.
  - **Context:** [feat: add return policy extension (dev.ucp.shopping.policy.return)](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/634), [feat: Introduce Lodging Booking Capability and Cancellation Policy Extension](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/780)
- **Topic:** SDK Strategy and Schema Tool Chain
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith introduced a deep-dive proposal for a UCP schema tool chain producing a reified intermediate representation, allowing types to be generated against a specific merchant or platform UCP profile. The group scheduled the deep dive for Thursday morning and deferred the Monday slot.
  - **Context:** [RFC: Interface Definition Strategy](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/800), [RFC: Decoupling SDK DTOs from Semantic Validation](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/817)
