## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** Feeds Domain Working Group Progress
  - **Notes:** James Andersen provided an update on the feeds domain working group, noting alignment on surfacing capabilities, lists of available feeds, and manifests for the December release. Custom providers and formats were agreed upon to solve both cold start and warm start challenges.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik explained that separating common architecture from shopping provides a generic primitive for feed discovery across domains while allowing individual domains like shopping to define specific record types such as products. James Andersen noted that identified schema gaps and conflicting definitions will be brought to the Shopping Tech Council for review. Greg Smith confirmed that formal draft specifications will be published for review in the coming weeks.
  - **Decision:** The domain working group aligned on separating common and shopping architecture to enable custom providers and record types across verticals.
  - **Context:** [feat: [DRAFT] feed capability for bulk catalog access](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/866)
- **Topic:** Direct-to-Consumer Proposals and Food Domain Feedback
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith reminded participants that Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) proposals for business-to-business (B2B) services and food are open for review ahead of an approaching governance deadline.
  - **Discussion:** Jing Li confirmed plans to respond to Ilya Grigorik's review feedback on the food pull request by end-of-day or early Monday, offering to schedule a deep dive if needed. Drew Olson inquired about decision-making authority for new DTC issues, and Greg Smith clarified that final approvals are governed through the Governing Council while seeking input from shopping council members.
  - **Context:** [feat: Introduce Food Checkout Capability and Fulfillment Extension](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/897), [[DTC Charter]: B2B Procurement](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/947)
- **Topic:** Accelerated Identity Proposal
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith previewed the accelerated identity proposal scheduled for a Monday deep dive, noting collaborative draft contributions from James Andersen, Ilya Grigorik, and Amit Handa.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik raised meta-concerns regarding protocol maturity, cautioning that standardizing complex delegated authorization patterns prematurely introduces unverified assumptions around OpenID Connect (OIDC) claims, whereas simple use cases can be resolved with minimal surface area. Greg Smith and James Andersen concurred on evaluating a downscoped pragmatic resolution, with Google identity specialists joining Monday to finalize direction.
  - **Context:** [feat(identity-linking): allow email matching when the IdP is the DNS-delegated authoritative email issuer](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/876), [docs(identity-linking): don't presume a client registration model](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/985)
- **Topic:** Business Identity Schema RFC
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith introduced a Request for Comments (RFC) pull request proposing schema updates to support business-level profile information independent of physical locations.
  - **Discussion:** Greg Smith requested that participants conduct a preliminary review of the proposed profile attributes and schema shapes ahead of detailed council discussion.
  - **Context:** [feat: [RFC] Business Identity](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/939)
- **Topic:** GitHub Projects Workflow Proposal for Technical Committees
  - **Notes:** Federico D'Amato presented a three-step GitHub project workflow proposal to streamline roadmap management, meeting agenda structuring, and external community engagement across technical committees.
  - **Discussion:** Federico D'Amato detailed the workflow consisting of escalation labels (`gov:tc-meeting`), GitHub project boards capped at five to seven agenda items per session, and automated circle-back syncing from meeting minutes back into pull requests and issues. Drew Olson supported adopting the framework across newly formed technical committees like Payments. Ilya Grigorik endorsed transparency while emphasizing the need to protect committee bandwidth by capping parallel workstreams. Amit Handa, James Andersen, and Greg Smith aligned on maintaining public read-only visibility for external community members while piloting the workflow within the Shopping Tech Council.
  - **Decision:** The Shopping Tech Council agreed to pilot the three-step GitHub project workflow and maintain read-only visibility for the public dashboard.
  - **Context:** [feat(ci): add meeting minutes circle-back workflow and script](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/meeting-minutes/pull/49), [feat(ci): add meeting minutes structure validator workflow and script](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/meeting-minutes/pull/50)

## 2. Open Discussions & PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Cart, Checkout, and Order List Price Extension
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith introduced issue 751 submitted by TikTok requesting the inclusion of `list_price` across cart, checkout, and order schemas to render strikethrough versus actual pricing without secondary lookups.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik argued that an order should capture the finalized contract as a complete snapshot of checkout rather than introducing piecemeal fields. Drew Olson, Julia Downey, and Jing Li expressed concern regarding persistence overhead, backend order management compatibility, and data freshness if businesses store current catalog values rather than point-in-time snapshots. Amit Handa noted potential complications with discount allocations and order totals. James Andersen and Greg Smith advocated for normative language making list price support strictly optional for merchants rather than mandatory.
  - **Decision:** The group agreed that list price inclusion should be defined as an optional field rather than obligatory.
  - **Context:** [[Proposal]: Return `list_price` in Cart, Checkout, and Order item responses](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/751)
- **Topic:** Return Policy Extensions Merge
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa confirmed that descriptions for the return policy extensions pull request had been updated.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik reported that recent updates addressed outstanding feedback and recommended merging following a final review. Greg Smith, Amit Handa, and Jing Li aligned on proceeding with merging the pull request.
  - **Context:** [feat: add return policy extension (dev.ucp.shopping.policy.return)](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/634), [feat(return): explicit eligibility, business-stated deadlines, typed fees](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/966)
- **Topic:** Purchase Option Extensions Update
  - **Notes:** Drew Olson reported that the Payments Tech Council reviewed the consolidated purchase options and subscription policy pull request 890, noting that Daniel Wyckoff considers the design nearly complete.
  - **Discussion:** Drew Olson recommended that council members review the outstanding changes to determine readiness for merging.
  - **Context:** [feat: add purchase options extension and subscription policy](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/890)
- **Topic:** Catalog Purpose and Reference Identifiers
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa and Ilya Grigorik reviewed progress from the previous deep dive regarding `purpose` and `reference_ids` in catalog search and item lookup.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik highlighted agreement that recommendation and swap capabilities should be modeled as an explicit extension rather than an implicitly negotiated shadow contract. Amit Handa questioned whether recommendation results alter the existing product list search schema, suggesting lookup and search operations could be decoupled. Both aligned that the proposal requires further refinement.
  - **Context:** [feat(catalog): add purpose and reference_ids to catalog search](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/902), [feat(catalog): similarity search, recommendations & substitute discovery](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/974)
- **Topic:** Agent-to-User Interface and Model Context Protocol App Bindings
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa and Ilya Grigorik discussed the Application-to-User Interface (A2UI) and Model Context Protocol (MCP) app bindings design sketch.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik reported findings from AgentCon, highlighting that MCP apps negotiate user interface support natively at the transport layer, suggesting UCP should provide high-level transport guidance rather than embedding tight bindings or version 0.9 quirks into the core specification. Amit Handa agreed to update the design sketch to align with version 1.0 specifications and decouple transport-level details.
  - **Context:** [feat: add UI extension with A2UI and MCP Apps bindings](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/899)
- **Topic:** Bundles Extension
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik plugged the open pull request defining bundle semantics for nested line items, requesting feedback from the council.
  - **Context:** [docs(checkout): define bundle semantics for nested line items](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/976)
- **Topic:** Card-Linked Offers Coordination
  - **Notes:** James Andersen inquired whether the Payments Tech Council plans to standardize visibility for card-linked merchant offers within checkout flows.
  - **Discussion:** Drew Olson noted that the topic has not yet been addressed in payments and agreed to place it on the next Payments Tech Council agenda to coordinate cross-council responsibilities.
