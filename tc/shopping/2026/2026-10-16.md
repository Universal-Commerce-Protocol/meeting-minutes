## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** DTC Expansion and B2B Procurement Charter
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith highlighted the expansion of Domain Technical Councils (DTCs), introducing issue 947 submitted by Amazon for business-to-business (B2B) procurement. Amit Handa outlined the formal governance process for public charters, explaining that the Governing Council reviews submissions, marks them as in progress, opens public nominations for candidate participants, and approves final composition before official DTC proceedings commence.
  - **Context:** [[DTC Charter]: B2B Procurement](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/947)
- **Topic:** Services Domain Technical Council Scope and Charter
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa and Prasad Wangikar clarified that the proposed services DTC will operate as an independent, self-contained technical council in parallel to shopping rather than as a minor shopping extension, reusing shared primitives like payments and identity. Prasad Wangikar outlined the services charter, noting it addresses scheduling and appointment booking across aggregator platforms such as Wix.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik sought clarification on whether services should be structured as a Domain Working Group (DWG) or a DTC, noting that DWG deliverables get absorbed into existing councils whereas DTCs represent permanent, independent swim lanes. Amit Handa noted that the donations initiative will operate as a scoped DWG under shopping to deliver recommendations and findings, whereas services represents an independent DTC.
  - **Decision:** The group aligned that the services Domain Technical Council will operate as a self-contained, independent swim lane in parallel to shopping rather than as a minor shopping extension.
  - **Context:** [[DTC Charter]: Local Services](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/922), [Domain Working Group (DWG) Charter: Agentic Donations](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/924)
- **Topic:** Milestone Tracking and Backlog Burndown
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith reviewed the December milestone timeline, noting approximately 40 working days remain to prioritize stalled protocol items. Amit Handa and Greg Smith agreed to clean up the milestone view and provide a granular backlog breakdown ahead of release planning.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik reported that product add-ons and bundles are active workstreams at Shopify ready to be externalized, while the availability Request for Comments (RFC) is straightforward and ready to publish. Ilya Grigorik and Amit Handa agreed to reserve dedicated deep dive sessions for returns and search recommendations. Ilya Grigorik expressed skepticism about premature complex GitHub tracking tooling, cautioning that the current view contains uncommitted large-scale items.
  - **Context:** [Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp)
- **Topic:** Risk Signals Propagation and Standardization
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith raised stalled progress on risk signals propagation (RFC 630). Prasad Wangikar suggested compiling a version one list of agreed risk signals from member input, noting previous discussions on definitions and device identifiers.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik argued against standardizing a monolithic risk taxonomy within Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), emphasizing that the protocol is extensible and allowing companies to define proprietary signal lists avoids endless debates over fields where past audits only aligned on IP addresses. Drew Olson observed that defining large schemas composed entirely of optional fields undermines schema rigor. Amit Handa, Drew Olson, and Greg Smith agreed to hold a dedicated payments deep dive to evaluate risk signal requirements.
  - **Context:** [RFC: Expanding Well-Known Signals for Agent-Originated Commerce](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/630)

## 2. Open Discussions & PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Independent Business Profiles and Community Feedback
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith shared insights from an adoption review with TikTok, who requested structured business profiles independent of physical location to capture brandmarks, corporate contact details, entity descriptions, and marketplace seller-level data.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik supported the concept, noting multiple platforms desire a standardized "about page" abstraction for businesses. Greg Smith noted technical considerations regarding how catalog representations should model seller details without denormalizing data across product records, proposing a dedicated deep dive session.
  - **Context:** [Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp)
- **Topic:** Permalink Specification Reversion and Normative Guardrails
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith reported an administrative review miss where merged pull request 789 introduced unapproved normative requirements requiring explicit user prompts before cart modifications via permalinks.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik expressed surprise at imposing behavioral UI constraints on standard permalink mechanics and recommended reverting the pull request to reconsider intent. Jing Li announced that an automated lint rule is under active development to detect normative language changes automatically in future pull requests.
  - **Decision:** The group agreed to revert pull request 789 to remove unapproved normative constraints on permalink cart modifications pending further review.
  - **Context:** [docs(permalink): require explicit buyer action before cart merge](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/789)
- **Topic:** Return Policy Extension and Advertised Eligibility Windows
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik presented pull request 966, stacked on Amit Handa's base pull request 634, shifting return posture from catalog-time "guarantees" to an "advertised eligibility window" to better reflect retail constraints and European Union disclosure standards.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik explained that the update also refactors fee structures to leverage common primitives so businesses can express custom fees, such as eco-fees, on returns. Amit Handa agreed to review the suggestions and merge non-controversial adjustments ahead of a Monday deep dive.
  - **Context:** [feat(return): explicit eligibility, business-stated deadlines, typed fees](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/966), [feat: add return policy extension (dev.ucp.shopping.policy.return)](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/634)
- **Topic:** Cart-Only Checkout Creation
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik presented pull request 940, which relaxes checkout creation schemas to allow passing a cart ID without requiring an empty `line_items` array while preserving backward compatibility and cart precedence.
  - **Discussion:** Ilya Grigorik noted that client implementations frequently tripped over this requirement, leading Shopify to implement this relaxed behavior silently. Greg Smith agreed to review the pull request for immediate landing.
  - **Context:** [fix(checkout): allow cart-only checkout creation](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/940)
- **Topic:** Identity Linking and Maintainer Engagement
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith and Amit Handa discussed pull request 876 for identity provider account linking, noting that security and privacy implications warrant broad cross-organizational review. Ilya Grigorik confirmed Shopify has prototyped the Chrome proposal and will resume active reviews next week once team members return.
  - **Discussion:** Greg Smith highlighted community contributions from the maintainer of the UCP checker tool. Jing Li suggested reaching out via the site's contact page to invite the maintainer to the UCP Summit.
  - **Context:** [feat(identity-linking): allow email matching when the IdP is the DNS-delegated authoritative email issuer](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/876), [docs(identity-linking): clarify RFC 9728 authorization_servers issuer rules and multi-entry discovery](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/891)
- **Topic:** Application-to-User Interface and Model Context Protocol Apps Bindings
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa presented progress on pull request 899, incorporating architectural overviews, end-to-end flow diagrams, and a local reference server implementation for Agent-to-User Interface (A2UI) and Model Context Protocol (MCP) apps bindings.
  - **Discussion:** Amit Handa outlined challenges with action management and list representations in the current A2UI specification that require temporary bridging within UCP. Ilya Grigorik questioned whether the specification risks being overly prescriptive regarding UI rendering logic, while Amit Handa clarified that merchants retain UI control while UCP standardizes data bindings for lists and quantities.
  - **Context:** [feat: add UI extension with A2UI and MCP Apps bindings](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/899)
- **Topic:** Catalog Purpose and Reference Identifiers
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa presented pull request 902 proposing the addition of `purpose` and `reference_ids` fields to catalog search to handle item recommendations and product swaps via existing search infrastructure, noting spec rules within the schema were tightened with agent assistance.
  - **Discussion:** Greg Smith noted that this functionality represents a critical step toward addressing platform disintermediation concerns and invited council members to review the draft.
  - **Context:** [feat(catalog): add purpose and reference_ids to catalog search](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/902)
- **Topic:** Purchase Options and Split Payments Extension
  - **Notes:** Daniel Wyckoff presented updates to pull request 890, consolidating purchase options and subscription policies into a single pull request and incorporating community review feedback.
  - **Discussion:** Daniel Wyckoff highlighted an enhancement to the split payments extension allowing allowed payment method combinations to be dynamically advertised during checkout. Daniel Wyckoff committed to sharing implementation examples across the Technical Council and Payments channels. Amit Handa praised the consolidated design and agreed to conduct a final review once comments are addressed.
  - **Context:** [feat: add purchase options extension and subscription policy](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/890), [feat: Introduce the split payments extension](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/409)
- **Topic:** Food Domain Technical Council Draft Review
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa requested council review on pull request 897 for the food checkout capability and fulfillment extension ahead of formal Governance Council progression. Amit Handa noted an initial approval from Ilya Grigorik is needed to merge the foundational draft and open iterative refinement.
  - **Context:** [feat: Introduce Food Checkout Capability and Fulfillment Extension](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/897)
