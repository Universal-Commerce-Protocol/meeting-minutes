# UCP Shopping Tech Council (STC) Meeting - 2026-09-04

**Led by:** Amit Handa

## Attendance

- **Present:** Amit Handa, Greg Smith, Ilya Grigorik, James Andersen, Demian Raspall, Drew Olson, Jing Li, Patrick Jordan, Prasad Wangikar, Vic Duggan

## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** Personnel Update and Project Management Handover
  - **Notes:** Demian Raspall attended their final meeting before departing Google and the Technical Council. The group recognized Demian's role as the coordinating force of the council and as Master of Ceremonies for the weekly meeting. Demian confirmed the handover is complete, with the tracking spreadsheets and the GitHub tracker synced as of the day before the meeting, and noted they will continue to follow the UCP ecosystem as a user.
- **Topic:** Domain Tech Council Expansion
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa announced the formalization of the Payments Tech Council, joining the existing Food and Lodging councils, with all three becoming operational over the following one to two weeks. Interest in the Payments council was high enough that its scope will be extended through domain working groups for tokenization and Buy Now Pay Later. Drew Olson outlined the operational timeline, with introductory emails going out immediately and a first discussion approximately two weeks out.
  - **Discussion:** Amit Handa explained that open seats will deliberately be held across the new councils rather than filling the slate immediately, so that a larger and more diverse pool of partners can be represented in a future election round.
  - **Context:** [Announcing the new Payments Tech Council members](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/discussions/798)
- **Topic:** Cross-Council Operating Model
  - **Notes:** Prasad Wangikar asked how coordination will work for horizontal topics that span sectors such as shopping and payments. Amit Handa and Drew Olson explained that cross-pollination of membership, where individuals serve on more than one council, together with Governing Council review, will act as the safeguard for managing interdependencies.
- **Topic:** Bulk Feeds Domain Working Group Progress
  - **Notes:** James Andersen reported on the first two kickoff meetings of the Bulk Feeds DWG, which were scheduled across two time slots to accommodate different time zones. Ilya Grigorik noted the group is still in a discovery phase, having mapped the complexity of the domain, and must now move from divergent thinking to a specific, manageable scope for the next UCP release.
  - **Discussion:** Amit Handa emphasized that while the working group has autonomy, its output must stay aligned with the broader ecosystem rather than introducing new paradigms. Greg Smith and Ilya Grigorik clarified that the working group is not a closed cohort and that membership additions should be documented via the RFC so participation remains transparent.
  - **Context:** [RFC: A Bulk Product Discovery Capability](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/550), [Domain Working Group (DWG) Charter: Bulk Product Discovery](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/.github/issues/53)
- **Topic:** Working Group Note-Taking Process
  - **Notes:** James Andersen and Ilya Grigorik raised the need for consistent documentation across working groups. The group agreed to adopt the existing template and repository workflow already used by the Governing Council and Tech Councils rather than introducing a separate process.
- **Topic:** Roadmap Pillars and Release Timeline
  - **Notes:** Greg Smith introduced a draft set of roadmap pillars covering continued development of the shopping funnel — fulfillment, identity linking, returns, and location. Ilya Grigorik proposed adding a developer experience pillar covering SDKs and tooling, and the group identified WebMCP integration as a timely priority given industry adoption. Greg Smith and Ilya Grigorik agreed to target an early December or January release to avoid the holiday period, treating that date as a planning constraint distinct from the protocol roadmap.
  - **Discussion:** Amit Handa and Ilya Grigorik discussed the difficulty of driving UCP with intelligent models, noting the schema supplies structure but does not define domain models or state progression, and agreed the roadmap must carry tooling improvements to make these interactions more reliable.
- **Topic:** Ecosystem Registry, Discovery, and Endpoint Validation
  - **Notes:** James Andersen proposed a registry to track UCP adoption and simplify merchant onboarding, noting current discovery is manual. Amit Handa and Ilya Grigorik agreed the council should hold a clear position but must weigh building it directly against leveraging community-led solutions. Patrick Jordan and Vic Duggan raised how an agent can establish that a UCP endpoint is trustworthy and functional; Ilya Grigorik suggested the council could offer an official validation utility built on the canonical UCP schema binary to confirm conformance, distinct from any guarantee of end-to-end transaction success.
- **Topic:** Community Contribution Process and Roadmap Visibility
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa and Greg Smith acknowledged that the current handling of community pull requests is insufficient, with the majority of work authored and reviewed by the Tech Council itself, and proposed a more responsive framework for incorporating outside contributions. Ilya Grigorik and Amit Handa noted the roadmap's lack of public visibility discourages contribution, and proposed publishing it along with a feedback or voting mechanism to surface requests with real community weight. Ilya Grigorik separately highlighted that first-time contributors unaffiliated with partner organizations currently hit a wall during the contribution process.

- **Topic:** Shopify v2026-08-25 Launch Report
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik reported that Shopify has launched support for the v2026-08-25 release, allowing agents to negotiate the protocol. The launch surfaced some internal bugs but produced integration testing experience that will benefit future releases.
  - **Context:** [Announcing UCP Release v2026-08-25](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/discussions/775)
- **Topic:** Deferred PR Discussions
