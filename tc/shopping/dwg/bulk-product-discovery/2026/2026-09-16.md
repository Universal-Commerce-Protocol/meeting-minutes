## 2. Technical Discussions & Working Group Consensus

- **Topic:** Inventory data in the feed
  - **Discussion:** Zhaocheng Li proposed adding optional inventory parameters to UCP, on the grounds that inventory levels would improve search performance for specific products. Greg Smith clarified that the current omission of inventory quantities is intentional rather than an oversight: including them would encourage consumers to rely on a catalog discovery mechanism as an inventory source, which a point-in-time feed cannot serve accurately. James Andersen noted that the existing `availability` field already supports basic in-stock status, while acknowledging that a boolean-style status may be insufficient for B2B cases where order quantity matters.
  - **Working Group Position:** No change to the baseline - inventory quantities stay out of the core feed. The group agreed to consider an optional extension carrying inventory data for specific merchant use cases, rather than adding it to the core product schema.

- **Topic:** Non-JSON external feed specifications
  - **Discussion:** Alex Jansen flagged that UCP's JSON-based schema constraints are incompatible with many feed formats already in widespread industry use, which limits the protocol's reach with businesses that have existing feed pipelines. The group explored defining mapping layers that translate an external feed specification into the UCP product representation, rather than requiring businesses to re-author their feeds. Tejas Mehta suggested that platforms or intermediate feed services are well placed to maintain these interoperability adapters until UCP achieves wider adoption.
  - **Working Group Position:** No decision. A mapping/adapter layer maintained outside the core protocol is the leading direction. This is the same interoperability question deferred on 2026-09-09 under "declaring alternative feed sources" - see section 3.

- **Topic:** Static and dynamic feed profiles
  - **Discussion:** Vinod Kumar proposed splitting the UCP feed design into two profiles to improve configuration management: a static profile carrying core capability definitions, and a dynamic profile covering extensions and contextual data. The split follows the architecture direction agreed on 2026-09-09, where the handshake lives in the existing static well-known files and manifests are served from a dynamic endpoint. The group also settled where the capability itself belongs in the namespace hierarchy.
  - **Working Group Position:** Consensus on the static/dynamic profile split. Decision to keep the feed capability within the `shopping/catalog` namespace rather than promoting it to a common namespace.

- **Topic:** Versioning strategy
  - **Discussion:** The group confirmed that UCP capability versions typically align with the overall protocol version release, so a capability is versioned by the release that carries it. Members questioned whether feed specifications and extensions need versioning independent of the core protocol, given that feed schemas and extensions may evolve on a different cadence than the protocol itself.
  - **Working Group Position:** Current behavior confirmed - capability versions follow the protocol release. Whether feed specifications and extensions can be versioned independently is open and assigned for investigation - see section 5.

## 3. Open Questions & Items for DTC

- **Topic:** P0 versus P1 scope prioritization
  - **Notes:** Carried from 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-09, still not finalized. Endpoint security and URL delegation remain the proposed P0 and the parse-failure safeguard the proposed P1.
  - **Needs DTC input:** Yes - the group will bring the finalized P0 scope to the Shopping TC.

- **Topic:** Placement of the feed handshake and manifest endpoint
  - **Notes:** Carried from 2026-09-02. Partially resolved this session: the group decided the capability stays in the `shopping/catalog` namespace rather than moving to a common namespace. The broader services-versus-capabilities discussion in the Shopping TC still governs how the dynamic manifest endpoint is exposed.
  - **Needs DTC input:** Yes, once a concrete proposal exists.

- **Topic:** Independent versioning of feed specifications and extensions
  - **Notes:** New. Capability versions currently track the protocol release. Whether feed specifications and extensions can carry their own version, and what that would mean for compatibility guarantees, is under investigation by Greg Smith. This touches protocol-wide versioning policy rather than feed-specific surface.
  - **Needs DTC input:** Yes, if the group recommends a versioning scheme that diverges from the protocol release cadence.

- **Topic:** Support for non-UCP feed formats
  - **Notes:** Carried from 2026-09-09, now with a concrete direction: mapping layers or interoperability adapters translating external feed specifications into UCP product representations, maintained by platforms or intermediate feed services rather than by the protocol. No owner assigned.
  - **Needs DTC input:** Not yet - the group will return with a recommendation.

- **Topic:** Optional inventory data
  - **Notes:** New. Inventory quantities stay out of the core feed by design. An optional extension for merchant-specific use cases, including B2B order quantities, is to be considered.
  - **Needs DTC input:** No, tracked within the DWG for now.

- **Topic:** Scope and boundaries of contextual feeds
  - **Notes:** New. Contextual data is assigned to the dynamic profile, but the boundaries of what a contextual feed covers are undefined. A scoping proposal is assigned to Tejas Mehta and Vinod Kumar will share findings on the chat thread.
  - **Needs DTC input:** No, tracked within the DWG for now.

- **Topic:** Diagnostic and error-reporting mechanism
  - **Notes:** Carried from 2026-09-09, not revisited this session. Three candidates remain open - structured payloads over the existing `messages` mechanism, a generic feedback API, and a contact address in the well-known profile.
  - **Needs DTC input:** No, tracked within the DWG for now.

- **Topic:** Ad-hoc dynamic feed generation
  - **Notes:** Carried from 2026-09-09. Parked as a possible future capability on complexity and abuse grounds, and explicitly not part of the baseline.
  - **Needs DTC input:** No.
