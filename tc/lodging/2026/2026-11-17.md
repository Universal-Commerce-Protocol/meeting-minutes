# UCP Lodging Tech Council (LTC) Meeting - 2026-11-17

**Led by:** Colm Gallagher (Google), Sean Carapella (Amadeus)

## Attendance

- **Present:** Colm Gallagher (Google), Chris Torres (Google), Jing Li (Google), Sean Carapella (Amadeus), Akhil Kavuri (Expedia), David Lund (Booking.com), Devesh Arora (Marriott), Lee Graham (Hilton)

## Agenda & Discussion

- **Topic:** [Non-Identity Loyalty Membership Capture Proposal (Issue #1068)](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/1068)
  - **Discussion:** Sean Carapella presented the open proposal for capturing loyalty membership without identity linking during booking. Jing Li shared early Governance Council feedback questioning why this is not modeled as an extension of existing eligibility claims rather than a standalone extension. Sean Carapella committed to having Gary Habura attend next week's meeting to present and defend the design choices, with a pull request attached if available.
  - **Decision:** None recorded.
- **Topic:** [Lodging Booking Session Expected Total Proposal (Issue #1103)](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/1103)
  - **Discussion:** Sean Carapella introduced a new proposal allowing platforms to pass expected room rates so businesses can log, override, or synchronize out-of-date pricing. Jing Li questioned whether this is necessary given that businesses act as the merchant of record, while Sean Carapella and David Lund noted that expected-price passing is common across OTAs, rental cars, and flights to handle volatile pricing. Devesh Arora agreed to check internal options regarding OTA price passing. Jing Li critiqued extending `stays[].totals` because `totals` is intended for business-to-platform authoritative price breakdowns rather than platform-to-business expectations, advising that the design be kept separate. Participants also raised open questions on whether additional items besides total are expected, what specifically is included in the total (raw total versus total plus taxes), and how negotiation or support should behave when a platform cannot support the requirement or cannot support it on `create`. Colm Gallagher and David Lund agreed that a pricing accuracy gap exists, and Sean Carapella will relay feedback to Gary Habura for next week's meeting while targeting the December 8 cut if feasible.
  - **Decision:** None recorded.
- **Topic:** Fetching Identity-Linked Loyalty Outside a Booking Session
  - **Discussion:** Participants discussed the functional gap preventing retrieval of loyalty data (such as points balance and membership status) outside an active booking session for upper-funnel journeys. Colm Gallagher noted that Igor Levit is actively investigating this by building a lightweight common Identity capability that the Loyalty extension can extend. Because it is a common capability, the group will need to coordinate with the Governance Council and potentially other DTCs to align on a design. While Jing Li and Sean Carapella agreed this feature will likely miss the December 8 cut, Jing Li noted that backporting should be possible because it introduces a new capability without breaking changes.
  - **Decision:** None recorded.
- **Topic:** Pay with Points, Days, and Wallet Credit Integration
  - **Discussion:** The group explored handling non-monetary stay payments, including pay with points, days, and wallet credit. Jing Li shared insights from a UCP summit where shopping and payments technical committees discussed modeling points similarly to gift cards within payment objects. David Lund asked about overlaps with vouchers and gift cards and also raised wallet credit, while Sean Carapella described models where points act as a separate currency without exact conversion. Jing Li suggested grouping non-monetary payment methods together and proposed deferring discussions with the Payments DTC until after the December 8 milestone, targeting a March 2027 implementation.
  - **Decision:** Focus current priority on meeting the December 8 goals and schedule a design discussion with the Payments DTC after December 8.
- **Topic:** Post-December 8 Priorities and Work Package Planning
  - **Discussion:** Sean Carapella committed to proposing combined work packages in two weeks, giving the group time to review and align on priorities after the December 8 milestone based on council priorities and community feedback. Chris Torres and Jing Li highlighted ad-hoc community feedback themes for the March 30 cut, including structured cancellation policies and additional guest attributes such as nationality and date of birth. Chris Torres will prepare a community issue roundup for the upcoming meeting to merge with internal work packages.
  - **Decision:** None recorded.
