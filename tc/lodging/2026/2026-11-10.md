# UCP Lodging Tech Council (LTC) Meeting - 2026-11-10

**Led by:** Colm Gallagher (Google)

## Attendance

- **Present:** Colm Gallagher (Google), Igor Levit (Google), Chris Torres (Google), Jing Li (Google), Sean Carapella (Amadeus), Devesh Arora (Marriott), Lee Graham (Hilton)

## Agenda & Discussion

- **Topic:** Version 1.5 Feature Requests and Backlog Updates
  - **Discussion:** Colm Gallagher reviewed proposed v1.5 feature requests covering loyalty member benefits, account sign-in during booking flows, communicating guest preferences and special requests, and retrieving bookings after creation. For the loyalty benefits feature request, council members discussed folding general tier benefits into loyalty data retrieval while evaluating whether booking-specific benefits fit in `dev.ucp.lodging.booking.policies[]`, `dev.ucp.lodging.booking.stays[].rate_plan.description`, or a dedicated field. Participants noted that sign-in appears covered by existing identity linking and unlinked loyalty identification. Google rated post-creation booking retrieval as priority 4, and supported adding guest preferences and special requests for future specification despite lower initial priority. Devesh Arora requested additional time to review these backlog additions in detail.
  - **Decision:** Added the feature request for communicating guest preferences and special requests to the v1.5 backlog for prioritization, pending detailed partner review.
- **Topic:** Policy Schema Granularity and Root-Level Policies
  - **Discussion:** Sean Carapella raised a design concern regarding whether `dev.ucp.lodging.booking.policies[]` at the root level can handle differing policies per individual stay. Jing Li clarified that the policy schema includes an applies-to pointer referencing the targeted entry in the root JSON schema, resolving per-stay granularity without requiring schema hierarchy changes.
  - **Decision:** Confirmed no policy schema restructuring is required because root-level policy entries reference targeted stays via schema pointers.
- **Topic:** Next Release Loyalty Prioritization and Ownership
  - **Discussion:** Colm Gallagher reviewed priority 5 loyalty feature requests targeted for the next release: identifying loyalty membership without account linking and retrieving loyalty data. Participants aligned on Amadeus leading unlinked loyalty identification and Google leading loyalty data retrieval. Sean Carapella noted a pending check with Gary Habura regarding creating the loyalty GitHub issue, and Devesh Arora shared that Marriott prepared materials on retrieving loyalty data and plans to open a pull request to initiate collaboration.
  - **Decision:** None recorded.
- **Topic:** Public Meeting Minutes Publication on GitHub
  - **Discussion:** Colm Gallagher announced plans to publish peer-reviewed Lodging DTC discussion summaries going forward to the [Universal-Commerce-Protocol/meeting-minutes](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/meeting-minutes) repository.
  - **Decision:** Publish summaries of Lodging DTC discussions going forward to [Universal-Commerce-Protocol/meeting-minutes](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/meeting-minutes).
