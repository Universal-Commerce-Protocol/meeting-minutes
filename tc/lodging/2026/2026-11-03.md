## 2. Open Discussions & PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Loyalty Requirements Overview and Consolidation
  - **Notes:** Sean Carapella presented updates to streamline the Lodging loyalty requirements backlog by consolidating older points-related items and adding two new requirements covering loyalty enrollment and loyalty data retrieval.
  - **Discussion:** Sean Carapella explained that separate requirements for displaying loyalty offers during merchandising and shopping were removed or merged because once a loyalty member is identified (e.g., via account linking), no additional specification work is mandated to surface member-specific offers. Points-related capabilities were bundled into a unified requirement covering displaying offers in points, booking, and paying with points.
  - **Context:** [Loyalty Extension Specification](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/common/extensions/loyalty/)
- **Topic:** Loyalty Enrollment Requirement Scope
  - **Notes:** Sean Carapella outlined the high-level loyalty enrollment requirement, noting that travelers should have at least one flexible mechanism—such as implicit sign-up via a checkbox during the booking flow—to enroll in a loyalty program if they are not already a member.
  - **Discussion:** Participants agreed on supporting at least one enrollment mechanism during booking while leaving room to debate additional sign-up methods in future iterations.
  - **Decision:** The council aligned on prioritizing the loyalty enrollment requirement at a high level, requiring at least one mechanism to enroll a user.
  - **Context:** [Loyalty Extension Specification](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/common/extensions/loyalty/), [Lodging Booking Capability](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/lodging/booking/)
- **Topic:** Identity Linking and Authentication Mechanisms
  - **Notes:** Devesh Arora raised concerns regarding missing sign-in use cases for authenticating members and assistants when retrieving loyalty data.
  - **Discussion:** Sean Carapella and Jing Li explained that account linking using OAuth 2.0 or custom Identity Provider (IdP) tokens handles authenticated flows between platforms and businesses. Jing Li also noted that unconfirmed platform claims could theoretically provide a conditional, unverified preview of loyalty data.
  - **Context:** [Identity Linking Specification](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/common/identity-linking/)
- **Topic:** Loyalty Identification Without Account Linking and Booking Extension (December 8th Priority)
  - **Notes:** Sean Carapella discussed a fallback requirement allowing provisional loyalty identification (passing a member ID during booking without full account linking) for booking flows where partners cannot support standard identity linking.
  - **Discussion:** Colm Gallagher and Sean Carapella agreed that this unlinked fallback must be strictly limited to earning points, and Sean Carapella noted that Gary is preparing a pull request and documentation outlining why eligibility claims do not work for broader loyalty flows. Evaluating priority items for the December 8 release, Colm Gallagher and Sean Carapella confirmed that adding the loyalty extension to the booking capability is critical.
  - **Decision:** The council agreed to limit the scope of loyalty identification without account linking strictly to earning points and to pursue the loyalty extension for booking for the December 8 release.
  - **Context:** [Loyalty Extension Specification](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/common/extensions/loyalty/), [Lodging Booking Capability](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/lodging/booking/)
- **Topic:** Post-Booking Retrieval and Member Tier Benefits
  - **Notes:** Colm Gallagher proposed adding a requirement for post-booking recall to allow users to retrieve past reservations directly through conversational assistants, while Devesh Arora highlighted the need to support member tier recognition and communication of complimentary property benefits.
  - **Discussion:** Jing Li recommended using the common `ask` capability for static property FAQs, and Colm Gallagher noted that member tiers and custom benefit text fields are already partially outlined in the existing loyalty extension specification.
  - **Context:** [Common Ask Capability](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/common/ask/), [Loyalty Extension Specification](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/common/extensions/loyalty/)
