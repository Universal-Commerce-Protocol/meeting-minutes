## Attendance

## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** Simon Farrow from WorldPay (now Global Payments) formally joining the Payments Technical Council, in place of Harsh Mehta
- **Topic:** **Pull Request Review Norms and Feedback Process**
  - **Notes:** Drew Olson and Amit Handa established review norms for pull requests, advising participants to keep ongoing work in a draft state for early strategic feedback, leave non-blocking versus blocking comments inline, and provide approval thumbs-up signals to clarify review status

## 2. Open Discussions & DTC PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Roadmap Discussions
  - **Phase 1**
    - **United States Payment Methods Support:** Drew Olson presented Phase one of the roadmap, focusing on supporting broader United States payment instruments such as e-wallets, DPANs, and Buy Now Pay Later solutions. Simon Farrow questioned whether instruments would be added to the Google Pay handler or require separate tokenization handlers, prompting Drew Olson and Daniel Wyckoff to clarify that payment handlers define their own schemas while balancing flexibility and consistency to prevent integrators from facing an explosion of custom configurations.
    - **Challenge and Security Flows:** Drew Olson and Daniel Wyckoff discussed pressure testing the generic actions design to support challenge and security flows like 3DS, noting that while an initial version one of 3DS was shipped, state machine limitations prevent updating checkouts once in progress, requiring further design improvements.
    - **Risk Data Standardization:** Drew Olson addressed risk data standardization, explaining that initial versions used an open key-value bucket and raising strategic questions on how to standardize risk data while balancing transparency versus opacity for tokens
  - **Future Roadmap Phases**
    - Outlined Phase 2 goals covering global expansion, split payments, agentic tokens, and loyalty constructs
    - Positioned Phase 3 for long-term preparations involving EU legislation, agent remittances, and agent-to-agent flows

- **Topic:** Domain Working Groups
  - Proposed spinning up a BNPL DWG and a Tokenization DWG to address complex domain-specific challenges.
  - Noted that DWGs operate with finite timelines and specific problem boundaries before reporting findings back to the PTC.
