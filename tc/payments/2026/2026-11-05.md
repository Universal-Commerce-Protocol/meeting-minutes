## 2. Open Discussions & DTC PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Offline Payment Handler Proposal Overview
  - **Notes:** Rose Wiegley presented a proposal for an off-site payment handler and off-site payment instrument to cover scenarios ranging from physical cash handoffs to external payment links like Affirm. Rose Wiegley raised an open design question regarding whether a merchant should use a single payment handler that surfaces multiple unselected instruments (such as cash, checks, or payment links) or if that blurs the definition of a payment instrument. Drew Olson responded that their perspective favors a single payment handler with an instrument per representation containing unique identifiers and terms.

- **Topic:** Technical Mechanics of Offline Payment Instruments
  - **Notes:** Kelly Siedl inquired whether account rails (such as instant payment redirects versus separate rails for European Union bank accounts) need to be modeled within the payment instrument definition. Drew Olson emphasized that offline payment instruments should remain intentionally underspecified as a catch-all bailout option to prevent merchants from forcing complex payment handlers into the flow, avoiding unnecessary complexity for users. Rose Wiegley added that the design relies on out-of-band updates once the user completes the transaction via quick response codes, bank deposit descriptions, or external links.

- **Topic:** Business Use Cases and Integration with Payment Terms
  - **Notes:** Rose Wiegley explained that businesses can choose whether to specify distinct directions for different offline methods or rely on third-party processor links. Drew Olson noted potential overlaps with the pay-by-invoice component and lodging domain use cases, suggesting that Rose Wiegley consult Daniel to ensure alignment with payment schedules and deferred payment terms. Kelly Siedl highlighted a Google subscription use case where users utilize out-of-band bank push payments initially before transitioning to merchant-initiated transactions, leading Drew Olson to suggest modeling this through split payments and distinct offline versus native bank instruments. Rose Wiegley clarified that credentials should never be submitted directly to Universal Checkout Platform for offline handlers, and agreed to share a draft with Kelly.

- **Topic:** Repository Structure and Invoicing Alignment for Payment Handlers
  - **Notes:** Rose Wiegley asked about the repository structure, proposing to place the payment handler under `source/schemas/top-level-payment`, which Drew Olson confirmed as a reasonable starting location. Edward Siok and Drew Olson discussed whether invoice-specific requirements, such as buyer address collection, should be integrated into a separate instrument or converged, with Drew Olson noting that the offline payment handler should act as a locked-down, restricted catch-all to prevent ad hoc implementations across handlers. Archana Malhotra and Rose Wiegley agreed to coordinate with Daniel Wyckoff to ensure no redundant controls are created regarding deferred payment terms.
