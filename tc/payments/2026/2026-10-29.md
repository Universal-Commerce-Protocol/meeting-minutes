## 2. Open Discussions & DTC PR Reviews

- **Topic:** [Issue 857](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/857) Deferred Payment Specification and Empty Payment Objects
  - **Notes:** Daniel Wyckoff raised a negotiation and specification issue regarding fully deferred future payments, such as lodging checkouts where no payment instrument or card on file is provided upfront. He noted that a documentation fix merged last month permits completion with an empty payment object, but the schema lacks programmatic communication indicating that a fully deferred payment term can be submitted without an instrument. Daniel Wyckoff and Drew Olson discussed that platforms would otherwise have to guess or parse description text, highlighting a gap in cross-technical council review before merging.
- **Topic:** Out-of-Band Payment Handler Architecture
  - **Notes:** Addressing the deferred payment problem, Rose Wiegley proposed introducing a manual payment type instrument or placeholder to cover cases like invoices or checks. Kelly Seidl added that out-of-band digital world payments, such as European instant payment pushes or crypto transactions, require carrying specific reference numbers for reconciliation through a payment handler. The group agreed on a consensus solution to design a simple handler representing these payment styles hosted on the `ucp.dev` domain. Rose Wiegley volunteered to draft a pull request, Daniel Wyckoff agreed to revert the previous pull request and add explanatory prose, and Drew Olson and Daniel Wyckoff committed to bringing the issue to the shopping technical council.
- **Topic:** [PR 987](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/987) Evaluation of Pull Request 987 and Open-Amounted Instruments
  - **Notes:** Daniel Wyckoff presented PR 987, which separates payment work from purchase options, creating a duality where unamounted instruments can mean either having no cap or being deferred for later. Jose Mendez expressed reservations about this dual meaning potentially causing confusion, while Daniel Wyckoff explained that open-amounted instruments avoid complex number-tracking problems across terms spanning time. Drew Olson suggested that Daniel Wyckoff create a simple gist demonstrating how the implementation would look with an extra bit to evaluate if the added complexity is worthwhile. Daniel Wyckoff agreed to provide the gist in chat, after which Drew Olson will re-review the pull request.
- **Topic:** Relationship Between Out-of-Band Payments and Invoice/Bank Transfer Proposals
  - **Notes:** Drew Olson noted that [Issue 778](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/778) and [Issue 957](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/957) regarding pay by invoice and bank transfers overlap with out-of-band payment handling. Kelly Seidl pointed out that bank transfers involve both pull and push mechanisms, with merchant-initiated transactions requiring different forms than invoices, indicating potential architectural dependencies that Rose Wiegley should review.
