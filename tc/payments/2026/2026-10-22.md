- **Topic:** PR Discussions
  - **Proposal for Separate Direct Debit Instrument Type [Issue 956](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/956):** Proposes a separate direct debit instrument type, aligning with the roadmap's first-phase focus on expanding supported forms of payment. Drew Olson assigned himself and Simon Farrow as reviewers.
  - **Proposal for Invoice and Pay by Bank Instrument Type [Issue 957](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/957):** Covers invoice and pay by bank options, noting that it addresses complexities specific to the German market as a post-pay option. Drew Olson added himself as an assignee to review.
  - **Purchase Options and Split Payments Extension Proposal [PR 890](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/890):** Daniel Wyckoff presented PR 890 addressing purchase options and subscriptions, explaining the problem of configuring required instrument types when vaulting is or is not supported, such as vaulting a credit card alongside advertising gift cards. He proposed leveraging the split payments extension to dynamically update and share available instrument sets based on whether a subscription is in the checkout, and provided a gist covering various edge cases. Additionally, he suggested using the open instrument concept from split payments to separate current payment amounts from open-ended vaulting candidates, and asked the room to challenge whether a second indicator for vaulting is needed. Drew Olson assigned himself to review.
